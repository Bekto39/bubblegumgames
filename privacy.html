Air Escape - Privacy Policy

Developer: Bubble Gum Games · Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Air Escape ("the Game"), published by Bubble Gum Games ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Game progress stored on your device - gold, pilot level and XP, unlocked planes, yokes and sceneries, missions, best score and sound settings. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.
  • Anonymous player account - to run the online leaderboards the Game signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services. This creates a random player ID; we do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Pilot name and scores - the pilot name you choose and your best scores are sent to Unity Leaderboards and are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your pilot name.
  • Purchases - in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (receipt) to deliver your item; we never see or store your payment card details.
  • Advertising data - the Game shows optional rewarded ads through third-party ad networks (Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads and its mediation partners, which may include Google AdMob). These partners may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.
  • Diagnostics - basic technical information such as device model, operating system version and crash information may be collected by the services above to keep the Game working.

2. How we use the information

  • to save your progress and run the Game,
  • to show the daily and weekly leaderboards,
  • to deliver in-app purchases,
  • to show rewarded ads and give you their rewards,
  • to fix bugs and improve the Game.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing - policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services, Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay - unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob (if used for ads) - policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Your choices

  • Ads personalisation - you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads). Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time.
  • Data deletion - uninstalling the Game deletes all progress stored on your device. To delete your leaderboard entry and anonymous player account, e-mail us at the address below with your pilot name; we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Data security and retention

Data sent from the Game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Leaderboard data is kept while the Game's leaderboards are active or until you ask us to delete it.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with its effective date at the top.
